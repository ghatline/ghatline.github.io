Fares

  • Fares on this website, including the fare estimator, are indicative. The final fare is the one confirmed in writing (WhatsApp or email) at booking.
  • Outstation trips are charged per km with a minimum of 300 km per day, plus driver bata per day.
  • Kilometres and time are counted from our garage and back to our garage.
  • Tolls, parking, inter-state permits, entry fees and GST (if applicable) are extra unless the quote says otherwise.

  • A booking is confirmed once an advance is received. The balance is payable as agreed in your quote.

Cancellation

  • Delays caused by traffic, weather, road closures, permit checks or breakdowns are not grounds for a refund, but we will always try to arrange a replacement vehicle.
