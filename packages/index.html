Tour plans

Road trip plans from Bangalore

Sample day-by-day plans our groups like. Change any day, add stops or stretch the trip, and we'll quote it on WhatsApp.

Coorg weekend

2N/3D~550 km round trip (approx.)10–26 seater

October to March is the best time to go.

Get this plan quoted

About the Coorg route

  1. Day 1Early pickup, breakfast on the expressway, Namdroling Golden Temple at Bylakuppe, Dubare Elephant Camp, check in at Madikeri.
  2. Day 2Abbey Falls, Raja's Seat sunset, coffee estate walk. Optional jeep to Mandalpatti.
  3. Day 3Talacauvery and Bhagamandala in the morning, drive back to Bengaluru.

Chikmagalur and Belur–Halebidu

2N/3D~520 km round trip (approx.)10–20 seater

September to March is the best time to go.

Get this plan quoted

About the Chikmagalur route

  1. Day 1Drive via Hassan, stop at Belur and Halebidu temples, check in at Chikmagalur.
  2. Day 2Mullayanagiri sunrise, Baba Budangiri, Jhari or Hebbe falls by jeep.
  3. Day 3Coffee museum or estate visit, return to Bengaluru by evening.

Hampi and Badami heritage circuit

4N/5D~1,100 km round trip (approx.)12–20 seater

October to February is the best time to go.

Get this plan quoted

About the Hampi route

  1. Day 1Drive to Hampi via Chitradurga Fort.
  2. Day 2Virupaksha, Vittala Temple, Lotus Mahal, Hemakuta sunset.
  3. Day 3Anegundi and coracle ride, drive to Badami.
  4. Day 4Badami caves, Aihole and Pattadakal.
  5. Day 5Return to Bengaluru.

Coastal temple trail

3N/4D~1,000 km round trip (approx.)12–26 seater

October to March is the best time to go.

Get this plan quoted

About the South Canara route

  1. Day 1Drive to Dharmasthala via the Shiradi ghat; evening darshan.
  2. Day 2Kukke Subramanya, then Udupi Sri Krishna Matha and Malpe beach.
  3. Day 3Kollur Mookambika, Murudeshwar and Gokarna.
  4. Day 4Morning at Om Beach, return via Jog Falls (seasonal) or Shivamogga.

Mysore and Ooty

3N/4D~650 km round trip (approx.)10–20 seater

March to June, October to November is the best time to go.

Get this plan quoted

About the Ooty route

  1. Day 1Srirangapatna, Mysore Palace, Brindavan Gardens in the evening.
  2. Day 2Chamundi Hills, drive through Bandipur–Mudumalai to Ooty (before the night closure).
  3. Day 3Botanical Garden, Ooty Lake, Doddabetta, toy train to Coonoor.
  4. Day 4Pykara on the way down, return to Bengaluru.

Kerala hills and backwaters

5N/6D~1,400 km round trip (approx.)12–26 seater

September to March is the best time to go.

Get this plan quoted

About the Kerala route

  1. Day 1Long drive to Munnar via Salem and Coimbatore.
  2. Day 2Tea gardens, Eravikulam National Park, Mattupetty.
  3. Day 3Drive to Alleppey; overnight houseboat.
  4. Day 4Fort Kochi, Chinese fishing nets, Mattancherry.
  5. Day 5Athirappilly Falls, drive towards the border.
  6. Day 6Return to Bengaluru.

Goa getaway

3N/4D~1,200 km round trip (approx.)12–26 seater

November to February is the best time to go.

Get this plan quoted

About the Goa route

  1. Day 1Early start via NH48; lunch at Hubballi, reach Goa by evening.
  2. Day 2North Goa: Fort Aguada, Calangute, Baga, Anjuna.
  3. Day 3Old Goa churches, Panaji's Fontainhas, South Goa beaches.
  4. Day 4Return to Bengaluru.
