Before you go: temple etiquette

  • Dress code: many temples expect traditional dress. At Guruvayur and Horanadu, for example, men wear a dhoti or mundu, and women wear a saree or churidar. We tell you the rules for each temple when you book.
  • Book darshan and poojas early: Tirumala, Sabarimala (Virtual Queue) and Kukke's Sarpa Samskara are booked on the official temple websites, often weeks ahead.
  • Phones and cameras: some temples, such as Meenakshi Amman in Madurai, do not allow phones inside. Leave them safely in the vehicle with the driver.
  • Early starts: darshan queues are shortest early in the morning, so we often leave Bengaluru before dawn.
  • Festivals: Dasara, Shivaratri, Karthigai Deepam and Makaravilakku draw huge crowds. Book the vehicle early and expect slower roads.

Frequently asked questions

Which temples can we visit from Bangalore by tempo traveller?

We run temple trips to Udupi, Coastal Temple Circuit, Tirupati, Dharmasthala, Kukke Subramanya, Sringeri, Horanadu, Kollur Mookambika, Melukote & Shravanabelagola, Male Mahadeshwara Hills, Mantralayam, Puttaparthi & Lepakshi, Srisailam, Tiruvannamalai, Kanchipuram, Madurai, Rameswaram, Velankanni, Guruvayur, Sabarimala, and we can plan any other temple route on request.

Can we visit Kukke Subramanya and Dharmasthala in one trip?

Yes. They are about 50 km apart, so a 1N/2D trip covers both comfortably, with an overnight stay at Kukke if you have booked Sarpa Samskara.

Do you help with darshan timings and dress codes?

Yes. We plan the day around darshan timings and tell you the dress code for each temple before you leave. Special darshan, pooja and Virtual Queue tickets are booked by you on the temple's official website.

Are tolls, parking and state permits included?

Tolls, parking and inter-state permits (Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh) are usually extra and are listed clearly in your quote.

Is the tempo traveller suitable for senior citizens?

Yes. The Force Urbania and the luxury 1x1 tempo traveller have the most legroom and the easiest step-in, and we plan regular rest stops on long drives.
