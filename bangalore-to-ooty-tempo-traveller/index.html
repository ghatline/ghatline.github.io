The route from Bangalore

Via Mysuru, Bandipur and Mudumalai. The forest stretch is closed to traffic at night (roughly 9 pm to 6 am).

Good to know: The Nilgiris currently require an e-pass for visiting vehicles; check the latest rules before travel.

Which vehicle should you book?

Most groups heading to Ooty book a 10–20 seater. For more comfort on the long stretches, the Force Urbania is quieter and smoother. For bigger groups and the best value per seat, choose the Force Traveller tempo traveller, or travel in style in a luxury 1x1 Maharaja.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Ooty from Bangalore by road?

Ooty is roughly 270 km from Bangalore, about 6–7 hrs by road one way. Actual distance and time depend on your pickup point, route and traffic.

Which tempo traveller size is best for a Bangalore to Ooty trip?

For Ooty, groups usually book a 10–20 seater. Small families often choose a 10 or 13 seater Force Urbania for comfort, while larger groups pick a 17, 20 or 26 seater Force Traveller.

What is the best time to visit Ooty?

The best season is March to June, October to November. The Nilgiris currently require an e-pass for visiting vehicles; check the latest rules before travel.

How is the tempo traveller fare from Bangalore to Ooty calculated?

The fare is the higher of the round-trip distance or the minimum km per day, times the per-km rate, plus driver bata per day. Tolls, parking and permits are extra. Use the fare estimator on this page for an indicative figure.
